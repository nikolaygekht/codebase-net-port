The audit measured the same port on the same table, through a harness that
read its queries from files rather than deriving them; these are the same
figures to within run-to-run variation, which is what makes this a baseline
and not a new measurement.

New here, and what sub-step 2 is aimed at: allocation per seek --
3 956 B on the character tag, 3 808 B on the numeric one. ANALYSIS.md 6
attributes 1 292-1 744 B of that to the key copied per comparison and
2 144 B to the byte[512] per block read.

For comparison, and the reason no absolute threshold can be a gate: the
identical run from WSL against /mnt/d reports 194 us per walked record and
1.58 ms per seek. That is the filesystem bridge, not the code.
